Matches

A match is three rounds, and each round is a fight to the death. Each round you win earns 5 K Serum, and winning the match earns a 20 K Serum bonus.

In 2v2, a side wins a round when the other side is out.

In Free For All, 3 to 10 players fight, and the last player standing wins the round. That pays 5 K Serum for each player in the room, and every kill pays 5 K Serum.
